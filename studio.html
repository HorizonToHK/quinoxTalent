AR STUDIO HK

@ar.studio.hk

The creative core of Quinox Talent. AR Studio HK is a highly versatile, multi-purpose production space tailored specifically to the needs of modern creators, idols, and commercial brands.

  • Commercial Shoots Fully equipped for high-resolution brand photography, product endorsements, and talent comp card creation.
  • Dance & Rehearsal Spacious flooring with full-length mirrors designed for intensive idol choreography and stage prep.
  • Video & Livestreaming Controlled lighting environments and backdrops perfect for dynamic video content, reels, and live broadcasts.
  • Audio Recording Acoustically treated zones integrated with our music production department for vocal tracking and MMO alignment.

MUSIC PRODUCTION & AUDIO ENGINEERING

Our proprietary "Music Production" database is meticulously organized to support live idol performances. Capabilities include:

  • SE (Stage Entrance): Custom entrance tracks designed to build hype before a performance.
  • MMO Creation & Stems: Instrumental tracking and mixing. We archive individual stems (Bass, Drums, Music, Vocals) for precise live mixing.
  • Lyric Localization: Proprietary Excel systems translating Japanese lyrics into Chinese and Romaji (e.g., matching lines to specific members like Funa, Hime, Ting).
  • Original Tracks: In-house creation of original IP songs, such as "Stardust with the Phoenix".
  • AI & Tech Integration: Utilization of advanced workflows to generate reference tracks, melodies, and automated pitch correction to streamline production.

VJ PALETTE & LED VISUALS

A live show requires visual impact. Our visual design team creates bespoke VJ palettes and high-fidelity LED wall graphics synchronized perfectly with our MMO tracks.
